How it works

1

Share

Pick On my way (live, with a destination and ETA) or Right now (a status like "At the gym"). Choose people and a time: 15 minutes to 4 hours.

2

They watch

They get a link by text, WhatsApp or email and watch your buddy on a map, with your ETA. They can send a ❤️ or 👋, or share their own location back.

3

It ends

When you arrive or the time is up, the share ends and your location is deleted. You can also stop it any time.

How Neerly is different

🔗

They don't need the app

Anyone can watch from a link, on any phone or computer. No account, no download.

⏳

It ends on its own

Every share has an end time. Then your location and trail are deleted. No history to leak.

🦊

A buddy, not a blue dot

Pick one of 16 buddies, earn outfits, and leave a trail. Watchers send reactions you see live.

🏁

They know when you're there

Drop a pin for an ETA. Neerly tells them when you're close, when you arrive, and (if you want) when you're running late.

✋

Consent, not coverage

No circles and no always-on tracking. Every share is a choice you make, every time.

🙈

No ads, no selling

We don't sell or share your data, and there are no ads or trackers. Read our privacy promise.
